# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Assuming good faith — most disagreements here are about trade-offs, not people

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and whenever someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by opening a
[private report](https://github.com/HilthonTT/nextjs-aspnetcore-clerk-starter/security/advisories/new)
or contacting [@HilthonTT](https://github.com/HilthonTT) directly. Reports will be reviewed and
investigated, and will remain confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or
contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
